Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TikTok Auto Publisher ("the Service", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when you connect your TikTok account to the Service. The Service is a personal automation tool that uses official TikTok Developer APIs.

1. Information We May Collect

When you authorize the Service through TikTok, we may collect and store:

  • TikTok User ID (the unique identifier of your TikTok account)
  • Username (your TikTok display name or handle)
  • Authentication information (access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, which allow the Service to act on your behalf within the permissions you granted)

We never receive or store your TikTok password.

2. How We Use Information

The information above is used only for:

  • Authenticating your account with TikTok
  • Publishing content to your TikTok account at your request

We do not use this information for advertising, profiling or any unrelated purpose.

3. No Sale of Personal Information

No personal information is sold, rented or traded to third parties. We do not share your information except as needed to communicate with TikTok's official APIs or when required by law.

4. Data Storage and Security

Authentication tokens and account identifiers are stored securely and protected with reasonable technical and organizational measures. Access is limited to what is required to operate the Service. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

5. Data Retention

We keep stored data only for as long as your account is connected to the Service or as needed to provide it. When you disconnect your account or request removal, the stored data is deleted.

6. Your Rights and Data Removal

You may at any time request access to, or removal of, the data we store about you. To do so, contact us at artemblonskij520@gmail.com. You can also revoke the Service's access directly in your TikTok account settings under the connected apps and permissions section.

7. Third-Party Services

The Service relies on TikTok's APIs. Your use of TikTok is governed by TikTok's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, which we encourage you to read.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed. Continued use of the Service after an update means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to artemblonskij520@gmail.com.
