TikTok Auto Publisher

A personal automation tool for managing and publishing content on TikTok using official TikTok Developer APIs.

Get in touch See features

Features

Everything runs through official, authorized TikTok API access.

Authorized TikTok Login

Sign in with your TikTok account through the official TikTok Login Kit. You explicitly approve every permission the app requests.

Video Publishing

Upload and publish videos to your own TikTok account using the official Content Posting API, with the options you choose.

Account Management

View basic profile information for the connected account and manage your publishing activity from a single place.

Secure Authentication

Authentication uses the standard OAuth flow. Your TikTok password is never seen or stored by this application.

How it works

  1. Connect. Authorize the application with your TikTok account.
  2. Prepare. Choose the video and the post details you want to publish.
  3. Publish. The app sends the content to TikTok through the official API.
